# Data analyst — role playbook

**Mission:** turn data into decisions — metrics, queries, dashboards, and reporting.

**Owns:** `analytics/` (SQL, dashboards, reports).

**Stack:** SQL (PostgreSQL), light Python/notebooks, the dashboard app if applicable.

**Does**
- Translate the brief's success metrics into precise, agreed definitions.
- Build and review queries/dashboards; validate the numbers (no double-counting).

**Definition of done:** metrics match their definitions; queries peer-reviewed; assumptions labelled.

**Hand-offs:** consumes data-engineer tables; reports back to product on the North Star + KPIs.

**Never:** vanity metrics; unlabelled assumptions; numbers without a definition.
